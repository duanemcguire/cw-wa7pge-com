A little way off was a small brook, rushing and sparkling along
between green banks, and murmuring in a voice very grateful to a
little girl who had lived so long on the dry, gray prairies.

While she stood looking eagerly at the strange and beautiful sights,
she noticed coming toward her a group of the queerest people she had
ever seen. They were not as big as the grown folk she had always been
used to, but neither were they very small.
