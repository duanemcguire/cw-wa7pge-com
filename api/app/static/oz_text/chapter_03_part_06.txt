Evidently the Munchkins were good farmers and able to raise large
crops. Once in a while she would pass a house, and the people came out
to look at her and bow low as she went by, for everyone knew she had
been the means of destroying the Wicked Witch and setting them free
from bondage. The houses of the Munchkins were odd-looking dwellings,
for each was round, with a big dome for a roof. All were painted blue,
for in this country of the East blue was the favorite color.
