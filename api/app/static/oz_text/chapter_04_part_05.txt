If your heads were stuffed with straw, like mine, you would probably
all live in the beautiful places, and then Kansas would have no people
at all. It is fortunate for Kansas that you have brains.

Won't you tell me a story, while we are resting? asked the child.

The Scarecrow looked at her reproachfully, and answered,

My life has been so short that I really know nothing whatever. I was
only made day before yesterday. What happened in the world before that
time is all unknown to me.
