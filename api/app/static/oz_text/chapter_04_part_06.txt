Luckily, when the farmer made my head, one of the first things he did
was to paint my ears, so that I heard what was going on. There was
another Munchkin with him, and the first thing I heard was the farmer
saying, 'How do you like those ears?'

'They aren't straight,' answered the other.

'Never mind,' said the farmer. 'They are ears just the same,' which
was true enough.

'Now I'll make the eyes,' said the farmer.
