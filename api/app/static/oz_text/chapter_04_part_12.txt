And she handed the basket to the Scarecrow.

There were no fences at all by the roadside now, and the land was
rough and untilled. Toward evening they came to a great forest, where
the trees grew so big and close together that their branches met over
the road of yellow brick. It was almost dark under the trees, for the
branches shut out the daylight, but the travelers did not stop, and
went on into the forest.

If this road goes in, it must come out, said the Scarecrow, and as the
Emerald City is at the other end of the road, we must go wherever it
leads us.

Anyone would know that, said Dorothy.
