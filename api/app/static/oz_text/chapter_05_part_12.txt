When I grew up, I too became a wood-chopper, and after my father died
I took care of my old mother as long as she lived. Then I made up my
mind that instead of living alone I would marry, so that I might not
become lonely.

There was one of the Munchkin girls who was so beautiful that I soon
grew to love her with all my heart. She, on her part, promised to
marry me as soon as I could earn enough money to build a better house
for her, so I set to work harder than ever.
