But the girl lived with an old woman who did not want her to marry
anyone, for she was so lazy she wished the girl to remain with her and
do the cooking and the housework. So the old woman went to the Wicked
Witch of the East, and promised her two sheep and a cow if she would
prevent the marriage. Thereupon the Wicked Witch enchanted my axe, and
when I was chopping away at my best one day, for I was anxious to get
the new house and my wife as soon as possible, the axe slipped all at
once and cut off my left leg.
