It was a terrible thing to undergo, but during the year I stood there
I had time to think that the greatest loss I had known was the loss of
my heart. While I was in love I was the happiest man on earth, but no
one can love who has not a heart, and so I am resolved to ask Oz to
give me one. If he does, I will go back to the Munchkin maiden and
marry her.

Both Dorothy and the Scarecrow had been greatly interested in the
story of the Tin Woodman, and now they knew why he was so anxious to
get a new heart.
