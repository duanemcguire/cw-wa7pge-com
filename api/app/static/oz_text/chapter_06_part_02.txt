These sounds made the little girl's heart beat fast, for she did not
know what made them, but Toto knew, and he walked close to Dorothy's
side, and did not even bark in return.

How long will it be, the child asked of the Tin Woodman, before we are
out of the forest?

I cannot tell, was the answer, for I have never been to the Emerald
City. But my father went there once, when I was a boy, and he said it
was a long journey through a dangerous country, although nearer to the
city where Oz dwells the country is beautiful.
