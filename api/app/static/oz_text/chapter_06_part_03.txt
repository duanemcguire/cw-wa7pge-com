But I am not afraid so long as I have my oil-can, and nothing can hurt
the Scarecrow, while you bear upon your forehead the mark of the Good
Witch's kiss, and that will protect you from harm.

But Toto! said the girl anxiously. What will protect him?

We must protect him ourselves if he is in danger, replied the Tin
Woodman.

Just as he spoke there came from the forest a terrible roar, and the
next moment a great Lion bounded into the road.
