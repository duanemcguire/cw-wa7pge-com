This made the Tin Woodman very unhappy, for he was always careful not
to hurt any living creature, and as he walked along he wept several
tears of sorrow and regret. These tears ran slowly down his face and
over the hinges of his jaw, and there they rusted. When Dorothy
presently asked him a question the Tin Woodman could not open his
mouth, for his jaws were tightly rusted together. He became greatly
frightened at this and made many motions to Dorothy to relieve him,
but she could not understand. The Lion was also puzzled to know what
was wrong.
