So get on my back and we will make the attempt.

The Scarecrow sat upon the Lion's back, and the big beast walked to
the edge of the gulf and crouched down.

Why don't you run and jump? asked the Scarecrow.

Because that isn't the way we Lions do these things, he replied. Then
giving a great spring, he shot through the air and landed safely on
the other side. They were all greatly pleased to see how easily he did
it, and after the Scarecrow had got down from his back the Lion sprang
across the ditch again.
