To their great joy the trees became thinner the farther they advanced,
and in the afternoon they suddenly came upon a broad river, flowing
swiftly just before them. On the other side of the water they could
see the road of yellow brick running through a beautiful country, with
green meadows dotted with bright flowers and all the road bordered
with trees hanging full of delicious fruits. They were greatly pleased
to see this delightful country before them.

How shall we cross the river? asked Dorothy.

That is easily done, replied the Scarecrow.
