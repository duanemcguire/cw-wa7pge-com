It was, indeed, a great yellow Wildcat, and the Woodman thought it
must be chasing something, for its ears were lying close to its head
and its mouth was wide open, showing two rows of ugly teeth, while its
red eyes glowed like balls of fire. As it came nearer the Tin Woodman
saw that running before the beast was a little gray field mouse, and
although he had no heart he knew it was wrong for the Wildcat to try
to kill such a pretty, harmless creature.
