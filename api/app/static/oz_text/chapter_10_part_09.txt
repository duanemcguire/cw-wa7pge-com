You see, Oz is a Great Wizard, and can take on any form he wishes. So
that some say he looks like a bird, and some say he looks like an
elephant, and some say he looks like a cat. To others he appears as a
beautiful fairy, or a brownie, or in any other form that pleases him.
But who the real Oz is, when he is in his own form, no living person
can tell.

That is very strange, said Dorothy, but we must try, in some way, to
see him, or we shall have made our journey for nothing.

Why do you wish to see the terrible Oz? asked the man.
