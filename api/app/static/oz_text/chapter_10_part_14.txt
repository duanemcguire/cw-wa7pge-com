Yet it was afternoon before they came to the great wall that
surrounded the City. It was high and thick and of a bright green
color.

In front of them, and at the end of the road of yellow brick, was a
big gate, all studded with emeralds that glittered so in the sun that
even the painted eyes of the Scarecrow were dazzled by their
brilliancy.

There was a bell beside the gate, and Dorothy pushed the button and
heard a silvery tinkle sound within.
