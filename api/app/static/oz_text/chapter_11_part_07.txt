It was the sweetest little room in the world, with a soft comfortable
bed that had sheets of green silk and a green velvet counterpane.
There was a tiny fountain in the middle of the room, that shot a spray
of green perfume into the air, to fall back into a beautifully carved
green marble basin. Beautiful green flowers stood in the windows, and
there was a shelf with a row of little green books. When Dorothy had
time to open these books she found them full of queer green pictures
that made her laugh, they were so funny.
