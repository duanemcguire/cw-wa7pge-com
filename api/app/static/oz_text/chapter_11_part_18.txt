You have no right to expect me to send you back to Kansas unless you
do something for me in return. In this country everyone must pay for
everything he gets. If you wish me to use my magic power to send you
home again you must do something for me first. Help me and I will help
you.

What must I do? asked the girl.

Kill the Wicked Witch of the West, answered Oz.

But I cannot! exclaimed Dorothy, greatly surprised.

You killed the Witch of the East and you wear the silver shoes, which
bear a powerful charm.
