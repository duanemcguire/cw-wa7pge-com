And then another crow flew at him, and the Scarecrow twisted its neck
also. There were forty crows, and forty times the Scarecrow twisted a
neck, until at last all were lying dead beside him. Then he called to
his companions to rise, and again they went upon their journey.

When the Wicked Witch looked out again and saw all her crows lying in
a heap, she got into a terrible rage, and blew three times upon her
silver whistle.

Forthwith there was heard a great buzzing in the air, and a swarm of
black bees came flying toward her.

Go to the strangers and sting them to death!
