With these words the Witch fell down in a brown, melted, shapeless
mass and began to spread over the clean boards of the kitchen floor.
Seeing that she had really melted away to nothing, Dorothy drew
another bucket of water and threw it over the mess. She then swept it
all out the door. After picking out the silver shoe, which was all
that was left of the old woman, she cleaned and dried it with a cloth,
and put it on her foot again.
