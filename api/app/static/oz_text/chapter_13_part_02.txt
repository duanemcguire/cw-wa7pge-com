They kept this day as a holiday, then and ever after, and spent the
time in feasting and dancing.

If our friends, the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman, were only with us,
said the Lion, I should be quite happy.

Don't you suppose we could rescue them? asked the girl anxiously.

We can try, answered the Lion.

So they called the yellow Winkies and asked them if they would help to
rescue their friends, and the Winkies said that they would be
delighted to do all in their power for Dorothy, who had set them free
from bondage.
