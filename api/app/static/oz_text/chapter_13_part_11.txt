Finding they were determined to go, the Winkies gave Toto and the Lion
each a golden collar, and to Dorothy they presented a beautiful
bracelet studded with diamonds, and to the Scarecrow they gave a gold-
headed walking stick, to keep him from stumbling, and to the Tin
Woodman they offered a silver oil-can, inlaid with gold and set with
precious jewels.

Every one of the travelers made the Winkies a pretty speech in return,
and all shook hands with them until their arms ached.
