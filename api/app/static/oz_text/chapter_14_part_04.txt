She sat down on the grass and looked at her companions, and they sat
down and looked at her, and Toto found that for the first time in his
life he was too tired to chase a butterfly that flew past his head. So
he put out his tongue and panted and looked at Dorothy as if to ask
what they should do next.

Suppose we call the field mice, she suggested. They could probably
tell us the way to the Emerald City.

To be sure they could, cried the Scarecrow. Why didn't we think of
that before?
