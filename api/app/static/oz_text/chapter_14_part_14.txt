He was dressed in a rich costume of pink silk and purple velvet, and
my grandfather thought he would see what he could do. At his word the
band flew down and seized Quelala, carried him in their arms until
they were over the middle of the river, and then dropped him into the
water.

'Swim out, my fine fellow,' cried my grandfather, 'and see if the
water has spotted your clothes.' Quelala was much too wise not to
swim, and he was not in the least spoiled by all his good fortune. He
laughed, when he came to the top of the water, and swam in to shore.
