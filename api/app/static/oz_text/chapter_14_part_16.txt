But my grandfather pleaded hard, for he knew the Monkeys would drown
in the river with their wings tied, and Quelala said a kind word for
them also, so that Gayelette finally spared them, on condition that
the Winged Monkeys should ever after do three times the bidding of the
owner of the Golden Cap. This Cap had been made for a wedding present
to Quelala, and it is said to have cost the princess half her kingdom.
