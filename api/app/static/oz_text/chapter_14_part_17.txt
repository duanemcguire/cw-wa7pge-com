Of course my grandfather and all the other Monkeys at once agreed to
the condition, and that is how it happens that we are three times the
slaves of the owner of the Golden Cap, whosoever he may be.

And what became of them? asked Dorothy, who had been greatly
interested in the story.

Quelala being the first owner of the Golden Cap, replied the Monkey,
he was the first to lay his wishes upon us.
