They looked again in every part of the room, and then, seeing no one,
Dorothy asked, Where are you?

I am everywhere, answered the Voice, but to the eyes of common mortals
I am invisible. I will now seat myself upon my throne, that you may
converse with me. Indeed, the Voice seemed just then to come straight
from the throne itself, so they walked toward it and stood in a row
while Dorothy said,

We have come to claim our promise, O Oz.

What promise? asked Oz.

You promised to send me back to Kansas when the Wicked Witch was
destroyed, said the girl.
