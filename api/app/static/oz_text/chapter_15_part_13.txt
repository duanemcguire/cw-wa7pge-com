Usually I will not see even my subjects, and so they believe I am
something terrible.

But, I don't understand, said Dorothy, in bewilderment. How was it
that you appeared to me as a great Head?

That was one of my tricks, answered Oz. Step this way, please, and I
will tell you all about it.

He led the way to a small chamber in the rear of the Throne Room, and
they all followed him. He pointed to one corner, in which lay the
great Head, made out of many thicknesses of paper, and with a
carefully painted face.

This I hung from the ceiling by a wire, said Oz.
