Of course I let them think so, because they were afraid of me, and
promised to do anything I wished them to.

Just to amuse myself, and keep the good people busy, I ordered them to
build this City, and my Palace, and they did it all willingly and
well. Then I thought, as the country was so green and beautiful, I
would call it the Emerald City, and to make the name fit better I put
green spectacles on all the people, so that everything they saw was
green.

But isn't everything here green? asked Dorothy.
