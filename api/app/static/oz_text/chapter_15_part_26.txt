I have played Wizard for so many years that I may as well continue the
part a little longer.

And now, said Dorothy, how am I to get back to Kansas?

We shall have to think about that, replied the little man. Give me two
or three days to consider the matter and I'll try to find a way to
carry you over the desert. In the meantime you shall all be treated as
my guests, and while you live in the Palace my people will wait upon
you and obey your slightest wish. There is only one thing I ask in
return for my help - such as it is. You must keep my secret and tell
no one I am a humbug.
