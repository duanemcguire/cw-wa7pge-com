Chapter 16. The Magic Art of the Great Humbug

Next morning the Scarecrow said to his friends,

Congratulate me. I am going to Oz to get my brains at last. When I
return I shall be as other men are.

I have always liked you as you were, said Dorothy simply.

It is kind of you to like a Scarecrow, he replied. But surely you will
think more of me when you hear the splendid thoughts my new brain is
going to turn out. Then he said good-bye to them all in a cheerful
voice and went to the Throne Room, where he rapped upon the door.

Come in, said Oz.
