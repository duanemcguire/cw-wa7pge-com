He put the heart in the Woodman's breast and then replaced the square
of tin, soldering it neatly together where it had been cut.

There, said he, now you have a heart that any man might be proud of.
I'm sorry I had to put a patch on your breast, but it really couldn't
be helped.

Never mind the patch, exclaimed the happy Woodman. I am very grateful
to you, and shall never forget your kindness.

Don't speak of it, replied Oz.

Then the Tin Woodman went back to his friends, who wished him every
joy on account of his good fortune.
