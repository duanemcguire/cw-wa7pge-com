But the first thing to do is to cross the desert, and then it should
be easy to find your way home.

How can I cross the desert? she inquired.

Well, I'll tell you what I think, said the little man. You see, when I
came to this country it was in a balloon. You also came through the
air, being carried by a cyclone. So I believe the best way to get
across the desert will be through the air. Now, it is quite beyond my
powers to make a cyclone, but I've been thinking the matter over, and
I believe I can make a balloon.

How? asked Dorothy.
