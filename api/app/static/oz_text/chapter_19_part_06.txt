In the morning they traveled on until they came to a thick wood. There
was no way of going around it, for it seemed to extend to the right
and left as far as they could see, and, besides, they did not dare
change the direction of their journey for fear of getting lost. So
they looked for the place where it would be easiest to get into the
forest.

The Scarecrow, who was in the lead, finally discovered a big tree with
such wide-spreading branches that there was room for the party to pass
underneath.
