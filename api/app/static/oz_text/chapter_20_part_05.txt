There were milkmaids and shepherdesses, with brightly colored bodices
and golden spots all over their gowns, and princesses with most
gorgeous frocks of silver and gold and purple, and shepherds dressed
in knee breeches with pink and yellow and blue stripes down them, and
golden buckles on their shoes, and princes with jeweled crowns upon
their heads, wearing ermine robes and satin doublets, and funny clowns
in ruffled gowns, with round red spots upon their cheeks and tall,
pointed caps.
