But here the country seemed wilder than ever, and after a long and
tiresome walk through the underbrush they entered another forest,
where the trees were bigger and older than any they had ever seen.

This forest is perfectly delightful, declared the Lion, looking around
him with joy. Never have I seen a more beautiful place.

It seems gloomy, said the Scarecrow.

Not a bit of it, answered the Lion. I should like to live here all my
life. See how soft the dried leaves are under your feet and how rich
and green the moss is that clings to these old trees.
