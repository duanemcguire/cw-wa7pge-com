The biggest of the tigers came up to the Lion and bowed, saying,

Welcome, O King of Beasts! You have come in good time to fight our
enemy and bring peace to all the animals of the forest once more.

What is your trouble? asked the Lion quietly.

We are all threatened, answered the tiger, by a fierce enemy which has
lately come into this forest. It is a most tremendous monster, like a
great spider, with a body as big as an elephant and legs as long as a
tree trunk.
