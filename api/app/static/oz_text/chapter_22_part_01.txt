Chapter 22. The Country of the Quadlings

The four travelers passed through the rest of the forest in safety,
and when they came out from its gloom saw before them a steep hill,
covered from top to bottom with great pieces of rock.

That will be a hard climb, said the Scarecrow, but we must get over
the hill, nevertheless.

So he led the way and the others followed. They had nearly reached the
first rock when they heard a rough voice cry out, Keep back!

Who are you? asked the Scarecrow.
