My greatest wish now, she added, is to get back to Kansas, for Aunt Em
will surely think something dreadful has happened to me, and that will
make her put on mourning, and unless the crops are better this year
than they were last, I am sure Uncle Henry cannot afford it.

Glinda leaned forward and kissed the sweet, upturned face of the
loving little girl.

Bless your dear heart, she said, I am sure I can tell you of a way to
get back to Kansas. Then she added, But, if I do, you must give me the
Golden Cap.

Willingly!
