Over the hill of the Hammer-Heads, he answered, lies a grand old
forest, and all the beasts that live there have made me their King. If
I could only get back to this forest, I would pass my life very
happily there.

My third command to the Winged Monkeys, said Glinda, shall be to carry
you to your forest. Then, having used up the powers of the Golden Cap,
I shall give it to the King of the Monkeys, that he and his band may
thereafter be free for evermore.
