Mumps one pound, that is what I have put down, but I daresay it will
be more like thirty shillings - don't speak - measles one five, German
measles half a guinea, makes two fifteen six - don't waggle your
finger - whooping-cough, say fifteen shillings - and so on it went,
and it added up differently each time, but at last Wendy just got
through, with mumps reduced to twelve six, and the two kinds of
measles treated as one.
