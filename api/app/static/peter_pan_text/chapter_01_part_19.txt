There are zigzag lines on it, just like your temperature on a card,
and these are probably roads in the island, for the Neverland is
always more or less an island, with astonishing splashes of colour
here and there, and coral reefs and rakish-looking craft in the
offing, and savages and lonely lairs, and gnomes who are mostly
tailors, and caves through which a river runs, and princes with six
elder brothers, and a hut fast going to decay, and one very small old
lady with a hooked nose.
