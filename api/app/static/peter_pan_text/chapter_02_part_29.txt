It is a punishment put on them for something they did so long ago that
no star now knows what it was. So the older ones have become glassy-
eyed and seldom speak winking is the star language, but the little
ones still wonder. They are not really friendly to Peter, who had a
mischievous way of stealing up behind them and trying to blow them
out, but they are so fond of fun that they were on his side to-night,
and anxious to get the grown-ups out of the way.
