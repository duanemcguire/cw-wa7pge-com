No more of it, Nana, she said sternly, pulling her out of the room. I
warn you if you bark again I shall go straight for master and missus
and bring them home from the party, and then, oh, won't master whip
you, just.

She tied the unhappy dog up again, but do you think Nana ceased to
bark? Bring master and missus home from the party! Why, that was just
what she wanted. Do you think she cared whether she was whipped so
long as her charges were safe?
