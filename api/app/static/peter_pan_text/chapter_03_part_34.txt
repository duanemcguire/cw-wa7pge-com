Unfortunately Liza returned to her puddings, and Nana, seeing that no
help would come from her, strained and strained at the chain until at
last she broke it. In another moment she had burst into the dining-
room of 27 and flung up her paws to heaven, her most expressive way of
making a communication. Mr. and Mrs. Darling knew at once that
something terrible was happening in their nursery, and without a good-
bye to their hostess they rushed into the street.

But it was now ten minutes since three scoundrels had been breathing
behind the curtains, and Peter Pan can do a great deal in ten minutes.
