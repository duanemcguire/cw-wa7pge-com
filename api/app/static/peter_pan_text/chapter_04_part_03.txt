His way was to pursue birds who had food in their mouths suitable for
humans and snatch it from them, then the birds would follow and snatch
it back, and they would all go chasing each other gaily for miles,
parting at last with mutual expressions of good-will. But Wendy
noticed with gentle concern that Peter did not seem to know that this
was rather an odd way of getting your bread and butter, nor even that
there are other ways.

Certainly they did not pretend to be sleepy, they were sleepy, and
that was a danger, for the moment they popped off, down they fell.
