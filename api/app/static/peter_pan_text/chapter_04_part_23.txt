Tell her to go away at once, Peter, the three cried simultaneously,
but he refused.

She thinks we have lost the way, he replied stiffly, and she is rather
frightened. You don't think I would send her away all by herself when
she is frightened!

For a moment the circle of light was broken, and something gave Peter
a loving little pinch.

Then tell her, Wendy begged, to put out her light.

She can't put it out. That is about the only thing fairies can't do.
It just goes out of itself when she falls asleep, same as the stars.

Then tell her to sleep at once, John almost ordered.
