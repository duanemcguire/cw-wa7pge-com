Here is Bill Jukes, every inch of him tattooed, the same Bill Jukes
who got six dozen on the Walrus from Flint before he would drop the
bag of moidores, and Cookson, said to be Black Murphy's brother but
this was never proved, and Gentleman Starkey, once an usher in a
public school and still dainty in his ways of killing, and Skylights
Morgan's Skylights, and the Irish bo'sun Smee, an oddly genial man who
stabbed, so to speak, without offence, and was the only Non-conformist
in Hook's crew, and Noodler, whose hands were fixed on backwards, and
Robt. Mullins and Alf Mason and many another ruffian long known and
feared on the Spanish Main.
