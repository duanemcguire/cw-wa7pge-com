Not until Wendy again raised her arm did he relent sufficiently to
say, Well, not for ever, but for a whole week.

Do you think Tinker Bell was grateful to Wendy for raising her arm? Oh
dear no, never wanted to pinch her so much. Fairies indeed are
strange, and Peter, who understood them best, often cuffed them.

But what to do with Wendy in her present delicate state of health?

Let us carry her down into the house, Curly suggested.

Ay, said Slightly, that is what one does with ladies.

No, no, Peter said, you must not touch her. It would not be
sufficiently respectful.
