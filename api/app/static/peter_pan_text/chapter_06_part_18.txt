Oh, really next I think I'll have Gay windows all about, With roses
peeping in, you know, And babies peeping out.

With a blow of their fists they made windows, and large yellow leaves
were the blinds. But roses -?

Roses, cried Peter sternly.

Quickly they made-believe to grow the loveliest roses up the walls.

Babies?

To prevent Peter ordering babies they hurried into song again,

We've made the roses peeping out, The babes are at the door, We cannot
make ourselves, you know, 'Cos we've been made before.
