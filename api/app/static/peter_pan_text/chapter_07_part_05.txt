There was an enormous fireplace which was in almost any part of the
room where you cared to light it, and across this Wendy stretched
strings, made of fibre, from which she suspended her washing. The bed
was tilted against the wall by day, and let down at 6,30, when it
filled nearly half the room, and all the boys slept in it, except
Michael, lying like sardines in a tin. There was a strict rule against
turning round until one gave the signal, when all turned at once.
