It was a sanguinary affair, and especially interesting as showing one
of Peter's peculiarities, which was that in the middle of a fight he
would suddenly change sides. At the Gulch, when victory was still in
the balance, sometimes leaning this way and sometimes that, he called
out, I'm redskin to-day, what are you, Tootles? And Tootles answered,
Redskin, what are you, Nibs? and Nibs said, Redskin, what are you
Twin?
