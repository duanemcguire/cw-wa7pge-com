A shorter adventure, and quite as exciting, was Tinker Bell's attempt,
with the help of some street fairies, to have the sleeping Wendy
conveyed on a great floating leaf to the mainland. Fortunately the
leaf gave way and Wendy woke, thinking it was bath-time, and swam
back. Or again, we might choose Peter's defiance of the lions, when he
drew a circle round him on the ground with an arrow and dared them to
cross it, and though he waited for hours, with the other boys and
Wendy looking on breathlessly from trees, not one of them dared to
accept his challenge.
