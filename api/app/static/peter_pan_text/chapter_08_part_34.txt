With a last effort Peter pulled her up the rock and then lay down
beside her. Even as he also fainted he saw that the water was rising.
He knew that they would soon be drowned, but he could do no more.

As they lay side by side a mermaid caught Wendy by the feet, and began
pulling her softly into the water. Peter, feeling her slip from him,
woke with a start, and was just in time to draw her back. But he had
to tell her the truth.

We are on the rock, Wendy, he said, but it is growing smaller. Soon
the water will be over it.

She did not understand even now.
