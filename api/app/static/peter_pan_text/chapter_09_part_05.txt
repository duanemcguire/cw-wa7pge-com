I - want - you - to - get - into - the - nest, the bird called,
speaking as slowly and distinctly as possible, and - then - you - can
- drift - ashore, but - I - am - too - tired - to - bring - it - any -
nearer - so - you - must - try to - swim - to - it.

What are you quacking about? Peter answered. Why don't you let the
nest drift as usual?

I - want - you - the bird said, and repeated it all over.

Then Peter tried slow and distinct.

What - are - you - quacking - about? and so on.

The Never bird became irritated, they have very short tempers.
