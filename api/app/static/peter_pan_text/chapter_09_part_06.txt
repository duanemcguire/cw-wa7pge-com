You dunderheaded little jay! she screamed, Why don't you do as I tell
you?

Peter felt that she was calling him names, and at a venture he
retorted hotly,

So are you!

Then rather curiously they both snapped out the same remark,

Shut up!

Shut up!

Nevertheless the bird was determined to save him if she could, and by
one last mighty effort she propelled the nest against the rock. Then
up she flew, deserting her eggs, so as to make her meaning clear.

Then at last he understood, and clutched the nest and waved his thanks
to the bird as she fluttered overhead.
