The Never bird saw at once what he was up to, and screamed her
admiration of him, and, alas, Peter crowed his agreement with her.
Then he got into the nest, reared the stave in it as a mast, and hung
up his shirt for a sail. At the same moment the bird fluttered down
upon the hat and once more sat snugly on her eggs. She drifted in one
direction, and he was borne off in another, both cheering.

Of course when Peter landed he beached his barque in a place where the
bird would easily find it, but the hat was such a great success that
she abandoned the nest.
