Then, he continued, in the short sharp voice of one accustomed to be
obeyed, Tinker Bell will take you across the sea. Wake her, Nibs.

Nibs had to knock twice before he got an answer, though Tink had
really been sitting up in bed listening for some time.

Who are you? How dare you? Go away, she cried.

You are to get up, Tink, Nibs called, and take Wendy on a journey.

Of course Tink had been delighted to hear that Wendy was going, but
she was jolly well determined not to be her courier, and she said so
in still more offensive language. Then she pretended to be asleep
again.
