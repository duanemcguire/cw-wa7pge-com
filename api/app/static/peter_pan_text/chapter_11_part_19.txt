And now, Peter, Wendy said, thinking she had put everything right, I
am going to give you your medicine before you go. She loved to give
them medicine, and undoubtedly gave them too much. Of course it was
only water, but it was out of a bottle, and she always shook the
bottle and counted the drops, which gave it a certain medicinal
quality. On this occasion, however, she did not give Peter his
draught, for just as she had prepared it, she saw a look on his face
that made her heart sink.

Get your things, Peter, she cried, shaking.
