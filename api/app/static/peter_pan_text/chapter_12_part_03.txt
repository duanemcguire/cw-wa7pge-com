Not a sound is to be heard, save when they give vent to a wonderful
imitation of the lonely call of the coyote. The cry is answered by
other braves, and some of them do it even better than the coyotes, who
are not very good at it. So the chill hours wear on, and the long
suspense is horribly trying to the paleface who has to live through it
for the first time, but to the trained hand those ghastly calls and
still ghastlier silences are but an intimation of how the night is
marching.
