Everything being thus mapped out with almost diabolical cunning, the
main body of the redskins folded their blankets around them, and in
the phlegmatic manner that is to them, the pearl of manhood squatted
above the children's home, awaiting the cold moment when they should
deal pale death.

Here dreaming, though wide-awake, of the exquisite tortures to which
they were to put him at break of day, those confiding savages were
found by the treacherous Hook.
