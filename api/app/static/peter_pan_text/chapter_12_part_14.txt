True he had flung Hook's arm to the crocodile, but even this and the
increased insecurity of life to which it led, owing to the crocodile's
pertinacity, hardly account for a vindictiveness so relentless and
malignant. The truth is that there was a something about Peter which
goaded the pirate captain to frenzy. It was not his courage, it was
not his engaging appearance, it was not -. There is no beating about
the bush, for we know quite well what it was, and have got to tell. It
was Peter's cockiness.
