Then one long gloating look he cast upon his victim, and turning,
wormed his way with difficulty up the tree. As he emerged at the top
he looked the very spirit of evil breaking from its hole. Donning his
hat at its most rakish angle, he wound his cloak around him, holding
one end in front as if to conceal his person from the night, of which
it was the blackest part, and muttering strangely to himself, stole
away through the trees.

Peter slept on. The light guttered and went out, leaving the tenement
in darkness, but still he slept.
