There were no children there, and it was night time, but he addressed
all who might be dreaming of the Neverland, and who were therefore
nearer to him than you think, boys and girls in their nighties, and
naked papooses in their baskets hung from trees.

Do you believe? he cried.

Tink sat up in bed almost briskly to listen to her fate.

She fancied she heard answers in the affirmative, and then again she
wasn't sure.

What do you think? she asked Peter.

If you believe, he shouted to them, clap your hands, don't let Tink
die.

Many clapped.

Some didn't.

A few beasts hissed.
