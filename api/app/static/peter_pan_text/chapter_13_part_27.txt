It was not such a night as he would have chosen. He had hoped to fly,
keeping not far from the ground so that nothing unwonted should escape
his eyes, but in that fitful light to have flown low would have meant
trailing his shadow through the trees, thus disturbing birds and
acquainting a watchful foe that he was astir.

He regretted now that he had given the birds of the island such
strange names that they are very wild and difficult of approach.

There was no other course but to press forward in redskin fashion, at
which happily he was an adept.
