But he never reached her, he never heard the cry of anguish he hoped
to wring from her. He heard something else instead.

It was the terrible tick-tick of the crocodile.

They all heard it - pirates, boys, Wendy, and immediately every head
was blown in one direction, not to the water whence the sound
proceeded, but toward Hook. All knew that what was about to happen
concerned him alone, and that from being actors they were suddenly
become spectators.

Very frightful was it to see the change that came over him. It was as
if he had been clipped at every joint. He fell in a little heap.
