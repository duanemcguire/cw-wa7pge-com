The crocodile was among those who heard the sound, and it followed
him, though whether with the purpose of regaining what it had lost, or
merely as a friend under the belief that it was again ticking itself,
will never be certainly known, for, like slaves to a fixed idea, it
was a stupid beast.

Peter reached the shore without mishap, and went straight on, his legs
encountering the water as if quite unaware that they had entered a new
element. Thus many animals pass from land to water, but no other human
of whom I know. As he swam he had but one thought, Hook or me this
time.
