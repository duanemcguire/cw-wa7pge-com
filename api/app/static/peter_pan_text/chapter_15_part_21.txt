Some of the miscreants leapt into the sea, others hid in dark
recesses, where they were found by Slightly, who did not fight, but
ran about with a lantern which he flashed in their faces, so that they
were half blinded and fell as an easy prey to the reeking swords of
the other boys. There was little sound to be heard but the clang of
weapons, an occasional screech or splash, and Slightly monotonously
counting - five - six - seven - eight - nine - ten - eleven.
