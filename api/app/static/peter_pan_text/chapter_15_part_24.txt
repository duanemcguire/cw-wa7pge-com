Peter was a superb swordsman, and parried with dazzling rapidity, ever
and anon he followed up a feint with a lunge that got past his foe's
defence, but his shorter reach stood him in ill stead, and he could
not drive the steel home. Hook, scarcely his inferior in brilliancy,
but not quite so nimble in wrist play, forced him back by the weight
of his onset, hoping suddenly to end all with a favourite thrust,
taught him long ago by Barbecue at Rio, but to his astonishment he
found this thrust turned aside again and again.
