Then he sought to close and give the quietus with his iron hook, which
all this time had been pawing the air, but Peter doubled under it and,
lunging fiercely, pierced him in the ribs. At the sight of his own
blood, whose peculiar colour, you remember, was offensive to him, the
sword fell from Hook's hand, and he was at Peter's mercy.

Now! cried all the boys, but with a magnificent gesture Peter invited
his opponent to pick up his sword. Hook did so instantly, but with a
tragic feeling that Peter was showing good form.
