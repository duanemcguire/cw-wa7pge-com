The other boys were flying around him now, flouting, scornful, and he
staggered about the deck striking up at them impotently, his mind was
no longer with them, it was slouching in the playing fields of long
ago, or being sent up for good, or watching the wall-game from a
famous wall. And his shoes were right, and his waistcoat was right,
and his tie was right, and his socks were right.

James Hook, thou not wholly unheroic figure, farewell.

For we have come to his last moment.
