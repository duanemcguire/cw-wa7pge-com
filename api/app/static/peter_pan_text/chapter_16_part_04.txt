The general feeling was that Peter was honest just now to lull Wendy's
suspicions, but that there might be a change when the new suit was
ready, which, against her will, she was making for him out of some of
Hook's wickedest garments. It was afterwards whispered among them that
on the first night he wore this suit he sat long in the cabin with
Hook's cigar-holder in his mouth and one hand clenched, all but for
the forefinger, which he bent and held threateningly aloft like a
hook.
