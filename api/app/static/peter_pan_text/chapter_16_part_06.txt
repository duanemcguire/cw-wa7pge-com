Even now we venture into that familiar nursery only because its lawful
occupants are on their way home, we are merely hurrying on in advance
of them to see that their beds are properly aired and that Mr. and
Mrs. Darling do not go out for the evening. We are no more than
servants. Why on earth should their beds be properly aired, seeing
that they left them in such a thankless hurry? Would it not serve them
jolly well right if they came back and found that their parents were
spending the week-end in the country?
