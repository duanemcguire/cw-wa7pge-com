I suppose it was because Wendy knew this that her last words to him
were these rather plaintive ones,

You won't forget me, Peter, will you, before spring cleaning time
comes?

Of course Peter promised, and then he flew away. He took Mrs.
Darling's kiss with him. The kiss that had been for no one else, Peter
took quite easily. Funny. But she seemed satisfied.

Of course all the boys went to school, and most of them got into Class
III, but Slightly was put first into Class IV and then into Class V.
Class I is the top class.
