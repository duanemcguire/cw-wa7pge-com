Peter came next spring cleaning, and the strange thing was that he
never knew he had missed a year.

That was the last time the girl Wendy ever saw him. For a little
longer she tried for his sake not to have growing pains, and she felt
she was untrue to him when she got a prize for general knowledge. But
the years came and went without bringing the careless boy, and when
they met again Wendy was a married woman, and Peter was no more to her
than a little dust in the box in which she had kept her toys. Wendy
was grown up. You need not be sorry for her. She was one of the kind
that likes to grow up.
