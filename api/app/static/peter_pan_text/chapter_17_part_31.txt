Yes, I know, Jane said, I have been waiting for you.

When Wendy returned diffidently she found Peter sitting on the bed-
post crowing gloriously, while Jane in her nighty was flying round the
room in solemn ecstasy.

She is my mother, Peter explained, and Jane descended and stood by his
side, with the look in her face that he liked to see on ladies when
they gazed at him.

He does so need a mother, Jane said.

Yes, I know, Wendy admitted rather forlornly, no one knows it so well
as I.
