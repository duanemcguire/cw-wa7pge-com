My Phrases

This is a phrase flow and Instant Flow Recovery (IFR) exercise running on your own phrase files. Go with the flow. Focus on forward motion. If a word is missed, ignore it. Focus on catching the next word.

Set the WPM and word spacing so that you are missing some but not all of the words as they flow by. That difficulty level will be the sweet spot for improving your flow recovery skills.

If you change the repetitions to 2 or 3, you may confirm your recognition by listening again.

Your phrase files are stored in this browser only. Use Edit my phrases to export a backup copy before you clear your browser data or move to another device.
