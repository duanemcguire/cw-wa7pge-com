She made herself rather cheap by inclining her face toward him, but he
merely dropped an acorn button into her hand, so she slowly returned
her face to where it had been before, and said nicely that she would
wear his kiss on the chain around her neck. It was lucky that she did
put it on that chain, for it was afterwards to save her life.

When people in our set are introduced, it is customary for them to ask
each other's age, and so Wendy, who always liked to do the correct
thing, asked Peter how old he was.
