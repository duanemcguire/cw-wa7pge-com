She poured out questions about them, to his surprise, for they were
rather a nuisance to him, getting in his way and so on, and indeed he
sometimes had to give them a hiding. Still, he liked them on the
whole, and he told her about the beginning of fairies.

You see, Wendy, when the first baby laughed for the first time, its
laugh broke into a thousand pieces, and they all went skipping about,
and that was the beginning of fairies.

Tedious talk this, but being a stay-at-home she liked it.

And so, he went on good-naturedly, there ought to be one fairy for
every boy and girl.
