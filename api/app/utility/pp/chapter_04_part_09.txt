He could go so much faster than they that he would suddenly shoot out
of sight, to have some adventure in which they had no share. He would
come down laughing over something fearfully funny he had been saying
to a star, but he had already forgotten what it was, or he would come
up with mermaid scales still sticking to him, and yet not be able to
say for certain what had been happening. It was really rather
irritating to children who had never seen a mermaid.

And if he forgets them so quickly, Wendy argued, how can we expect
that he will go on remembering us?
