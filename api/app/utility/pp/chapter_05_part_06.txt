Take care lest an adventure is now offered you, which, if accepted,
will plunge you in deepest woe. Tootles, the fairy Tink, who is bent
on mischief this night is looking for a tool, and she thinks you are
the most easily tricked of the boys. 'Ware Tinker Bell.

Would that he could hear us, but we are not really on the island, and
he passes by, biting his knuckles.

Next comes Nibs, the gay and debonair, followed by Slightly, who cuts
whistles out of the trees and dances ecstatically to his own tunes.
Slightly is the most conceited of the boys.
