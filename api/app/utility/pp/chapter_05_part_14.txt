In dress he somewhat aped the attire associated with the name of
Charles II, having heard it said in some earlier period of his career
that he bore a strange resemblance to the ill-fated Stuarts, and in
his mouth he had a holder of his own contrivance which enabled him to
smoke two cigars at once. But undoubtedly the grimmest part of him was
his iron claw.
