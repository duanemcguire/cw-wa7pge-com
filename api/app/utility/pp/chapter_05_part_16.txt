Strung around them are scalps, of boys as well as of pirates, for
these are the Piccaninny tribe, and not to be confused with the
softer-hearted Delawares or the Hurons. In the van, on all fours, is
Great Big Little Panther, a brave of so many scalps that in his
present position they somewhat impede his progress. Bringing up the
rear, the place of greatest danger, comes Tiger Lily, proudly erect, a
princess in her own right.
