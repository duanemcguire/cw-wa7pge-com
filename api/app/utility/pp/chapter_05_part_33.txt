Avast, belay, when I appear, By fear they're overtook, Nought's left
upon your bones when you Have shaken claws with Hook.

They began the verse, but they never finished it, for another sound
broke in and stilled them. There was at first such a tiny sound that a
leaf might have fallen on it and smothered it, but as it came nearer
it was more distinct.

Tick tick tick tick!

Hook stood shuddering, one foot in the air.

The crocodile! he gasped, and bounded away, followed by his bo'sun.

It was indeed the crocodile.
