Tut, tut, tut, he said, where does she lie?

In yonder glade.

I will put a glass thing in her mouth, said Slightly, and he made-
believe to do it, while Peter waited. It was an anxious moment when
the glass thing was withdrawn.

How is she? inquired Peter.

Tut, tut, tut, said Slightly, this has cured her.

I am glad! Peter cried.

I will call again in the evening, Slightly said, give her beef tea out
of a cup with a spout to it, but after he had returned the hat to John
he blew big breaths, which was his habit on escaping from a
difficulty.
