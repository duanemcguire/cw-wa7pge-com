He knocked politely, and now the wood was as still as the children,
not a sound to be heard except from Tinker Bell, who was watching from
a branch and openly sneering.

What the boys were wondering was, would any one answer the knock? If a
lady, what would she be like?

The door opened and a lady came out. It was Wendy. They all whipped
off their hats.

She looked properly surprised, and this was just how they had hoped
she would look.

Where am I? she said.

Of course Slightly was the first to get his word in.
