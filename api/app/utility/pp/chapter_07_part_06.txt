Michael should have used it also, but Wendy would have a baby, and he

It was rough and simple, and not unlike what baby bears would have
made of an underground house in the same circumstances. But there was
one recess in the wall, no larger than a bird-cage, which was the
private apartment of Tinker Bell. It could be shut off from the rest
of the house by a tiny curtain, which Tink, who was most fastidious,
always kept drawn when dressing or undressing.
