By working her wings, in a way she had learned since the nest fell
into the water, she was able to some extent to guide her strange
craft, but by the time Peter recognised her she was very exhausted.
She had come to save him, to give him her nest, though there were eggs
in it. I rather wonder at the bird, for though he had been nice to
her, he had also sometimes tormented her. I can suppose only that,
like Mrs. Darling and the rest of them, she was melted because he had
all his first teeth.
