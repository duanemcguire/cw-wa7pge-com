Peter Pan has spoken.

Always when he said, Peter Pan has spoken, it meant that they must now
shut up, and they accepted it humbly in that spirit, but they were by
no means so respectful to the other boys, whom they looked upon as
just ordinary braves. They said How-do? to them, and things like that,
and what annoyed the boys was that Peter seemed to think this all
right.

Secretly Wendy sympathised with them a little, but she was far too
loyal a housewife to listen to any complaints against father. Father
knows best, she always said, whatever her private opinion must be.
