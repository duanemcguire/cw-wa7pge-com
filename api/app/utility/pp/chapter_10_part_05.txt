The meal happened to be a make-believe tea, and they sat around the
board, guzzling in their greed, and really, what with their chatter
and recriminations, the noise, as Wendy said, was positively
deafening. To be sure, she did not mind noise, but she simply would
not have them grabbing things, and then excusing themselves by saying
that Tootles had pushed their elbow.
