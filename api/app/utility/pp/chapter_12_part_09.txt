They knew it, but as their father's sons they acquitted themselves.
Even then they had time to gather in a phalanx that would have been
hard to break had they risen quickly, but this they were forbidden to
do by the traditions of their race. It is written that the noble
savage must never express surprise in the presence of the white. Thus
terrible as the sudden appearance of the pirates must have been to
them, they remained stationary for a moment, not a muscle moving, as
if the foe had come by invitation.
