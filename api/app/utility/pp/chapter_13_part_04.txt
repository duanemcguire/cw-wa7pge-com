All went well until Slightly's turn came, when he was found to be like
those irritating parcels that use up all the string in going round and
leave no tags with which to tie a knot. The pirates kicked him in
their rage, just as you kick the parcel though in fairness you should
kick the string, and strange to say it was Hook who told them to belay
their violence. His lip was curled with malicious triumph.
