While his dogs were merely sweating because every time they tried to
pack the unhappy lad tight in one part he bulged out in another,
Hook's master mind had gone far beneath Slightly's surface, probing
not for effects but for causes, and his exultation showed that he had
found them. Slightly, white to the gills, knew that Hook had surprised
his secret, which was this, that no boy so blown out could use a tree
wherein an average man need stick. Poor Slightly, most wretched of all
the children now, for he was in a panic about Peter, bitterly
regretted what he had done.
