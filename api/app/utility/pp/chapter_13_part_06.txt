Madly addicted to the drinking of water when he was hot, he had
swelled in consequence to his present girth, and instead of reducing
himself to fit his tree he had, unknown to the others, whittled his
tree to make it fit him.

Sufficient of this Hook guessed to persuade him that Peter at last lay
at his mercy, but no word of the dark design that now formed in the
subterranean caverns of his mind crossed his lips, he merely signed
that the captives were to be conveyed to the ship, and that he would
be alone.

How to convey them?
