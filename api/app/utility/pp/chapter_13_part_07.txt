Hunched up in their ropes they might indeed be rolled down hill like
barrels, but most of the way lay through a morass. Again Hook's genius
surmounted difficulties. He indicated that the little house must be
used as a conveyance. The children were flung into it, four stout
pirates raised it on their shoulders, the others fell in behind, and
singing the hateful pirate chorus the strange procession set off
through the wood.
