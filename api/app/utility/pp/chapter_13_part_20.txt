Unlike Slightly's door, it filled the aperture, so that he could not
see beyond it, nor could the one knocking see him.

I won't open unless you speak, Peter cried.

Then at last the visitor spoke, in a lovely bell-like voice.

Let me in, Peter.

It was Tink, and quickly he unbarred to her. She flew in excitedly,
her face flushed and her dress stained with mud.

What is it?

Oh, you could never guess! she cried, and offered him three guesses.
Out with it!
