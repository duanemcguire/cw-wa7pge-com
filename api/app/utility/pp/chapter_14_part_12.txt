He lolled at his ease, humming, not unmelodiously, snatches of a rude
song, and fingering a pack of cards. Ever and anon the light from his
cigar gave a touch of colour to his face.

Now then, bullies, he said briskly, six of you walk the plank to-
night, but I have room for two cabin boys. Which of you is it to be?

Don't irritate him unnecessarily, had been Wendy's instructions in the
hold, so Tootles stepped forward politely.
