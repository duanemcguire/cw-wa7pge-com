To the boys there was at least some glamour in the pirate calling, but
all that she saw was that the ship had not been tidied for years.
There was not a porthole on the grimy glass of which you might not
have written with your finger Dirty pig, and she had already written
it on several. But as the boys gathered round her she had no thought,
of course, save for them.

So, my beauty, said Hook, as if he spoke in syrup, you are to see your
children walk the plank.
