At first he thought this eerie, but soon concluded rightly that the
clock had run down.

Without giving a thought to what might be the feelings of a fellow-
creature thus abruptly deprived of its closest companion, Peter began
to consider how he could turn the catastrophe to his own use, and he
decided to tick, so that wild beasts should believe he was the
crocodile and let him pass unmolested. He ticked superbly, but with
one unforeseen result.
