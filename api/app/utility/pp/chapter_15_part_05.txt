Then he realised that he was doing it himself, and in a flash he
understood the situation. How clever of me! he thought at once, and
signed to the boys not to burst into applause.

It was at this moment that Ed Teynte the quartermaster emerged from
the forecastle and came along the deck. Now, reader, time what
happened by your watch. Peter struck true and deep. John clapped his
hands on the ill-fated pirate's mouth to stifle the dying groan. He
fell forward. Four boys caught him to prevent the thud. Peter gave the
signal, and the carrion was cast overboard.
