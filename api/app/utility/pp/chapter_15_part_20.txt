Peter's voice rang out, and in another moment the clash of arms was
resounding through the ship. Had the pirates kept together it is
certain that they would have won, but the onset came when they were
still unstrung, and they ran hither and thither, striking wildly, each
thinking himself the last survivor of the crew. Man to man they were
the stronger, but they fought on the defensive only, which enabled the
boys to hunt in pairs and choose their quarry.
