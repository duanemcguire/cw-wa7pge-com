Peter had already lashed himself to the wheel, but he piped all hands
and delivered a short address to them, said he hoped they would do
their duty like gallant hearties, but that he knew they were the scum
of Rio and the Gold Coast, and if they snapped at him he would tear
them. The bluff strident words struck the note sailors understood, and
they cheered him lustily. Then a few sharp orders were given, and they
turned the ship round, and nosed her for the mainland.
