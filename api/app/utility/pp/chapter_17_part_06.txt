About me, Peter?

No.

Mrs. Darling came to the window, for at present she was keeping a
sharp eye on Wendy. She told Peter that she had adopted all the other
boys, and would like to adopt him also.

Would you send me to school? he inquired craftily.

Yes.

And then to an office?

I suppose so.

Soon I would be a man?

Very soon.

I don't want to go to school and learn solemn things, he told her
passionately. I don't want to be a man. O Wendy's mother, if I was to
wake up and feel there was a beard!
