It was Jane's nursery now, for her father had bought it at the three
per cents from Wendy's father, who was no longer fond of stairs. Mrs.
Darling was now dead and forgotten.

There were only two beds in the nursery now, Jane's and her nurse's,
and there was no kennel, for Nana also had passed away. She died of
old age, and at the end she had been rather difficult to get on with,
being very firmly convinced that no one knew how to look after
children except herself.

Once a week Jane's nurse had her evening off, and then it was Wendy's
part to put Jane to bed.
